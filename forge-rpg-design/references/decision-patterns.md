# Decision Patterns

Use these as precedents, not mandatory setting canon.

## Scope and ownership precedents

- Treat a Guardian as a selectable loadout or mode, not a summon or companion. Permit one active expression at a time, keep resources attached to their actual archetype or subsystem, and prevent switching from refreshing spent resources.
- Keep a reusable subsystem independent when its scaling and vocabulary stand on their own. Import it into a class through an adapter instead of rewriting it as class-owned content.
- Keep an Akashic Reactor-like engine as a class feature when it modifies compatible veils. Put compact compatibility entries on the veils; do not make the reactor itself a veil, slot, or shaping permission.
- Use a bind or higher-tier expression to externalize a veil's fantasy when that escalation matches the slot: mantle, area, ally support, socket array, or other visible output.

## Soul and scene precedents

- “Ninja” is too broad. A strong spine creates the scene: observe, plan, misdirect, enter, strike, exit. Options should occupy or modify beats in that sequence. “Rogue with shuriken” fails the scene gate.
- A visual anchor can settle design language before mechanics. An elegant silver gentleman weapon implies refined hidden danger, cane-first silhouette, clockwork glamour, and a transformation that preserves poise rather than becoming generic heavy machinery.
- A veil should sing before it is sanitized. Establish how it appears, moves, and feels; then repair DCs, binds, scaling, and ambiguity.
- A conversion should preserve the original chassis logic while changing expression. A cybertech Mahou-Ken remains recognizably Mahou-Ken rather than becoming an unrelated spell-delivery device.

## Spine and option precedents

- Treat Observe-like mechanics as a subsystem spine when many options can prepare, exploit, redirect, or conclude the observed state.
- Use tiered options when tiers change decision quality or risk, such as least/lesser/greater reagents with stronger effects and sharper drawbacks. Do not use tiers as labels for flat numeric inflation.
- Require foundational picks at fixed levels when later options assume their vocabulary or engine. Distinguish true prerequisites from optional accelerators.
- Use Cross Training and Advanced Training-like rules only with explicit effective-level scaling, resource treatment, and prerequisite boundaries. Avoid granting an entire foreign progression through a vague feature reference.
- Let modules occupy a stable interface: legality/tier, capacity or investment, reconfiguration timing, DC rule, and visible recompilation or manifestation. Open-ended effects still need a bounded collaboration rule.

## Common kill reasons

- **Costume-only:** The option carries the name or weapon but does not change play.
- **Wrong owner:** The rule belongs to another layer or steals a subsystem's identity.
- **Second spine:** The option creates a competing engine instead of supporting the core loop.
- **Free economy:** It creates extra actions, actors, initiative, flanking, items, slots, or resource refreshes.
- **Tax pick:** It is mandatory only because later math assumes it, not because it establishes necessary play.
- **Auto-pick:** Its benefit dominates every alternative without a situational cost.
- **Rename:** It duplicates an existing option with new flavor text.
- **Visual silence:** The mechanic may function, but the manifestation cannot be pictured.
- **Scaling leak:** It uses the wrong level, HD, modifier, DC, or multiclass rule.
- **Interface ambiguity:** It omits timing, capacity, bind, descriptor, stacking, or reshaping rules.
- **Explanation debt:** It needs paragraphs of exceptions to coexist with the chassis.

## Compact decision ledger

For difficult revisions, record only consequential decisions:

| Candidate | Verdict | Decisive reason | Needed change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direction A | Keep | Carries the scene and fits the owning layer | Clarify scaling |
| Direction B | Merge | Strong image, redundant mechanic | Fold image into A |
| Direction C | Reserve | Good fantasy, belongs to another subsystem | Save as adapter |
| Direction D | Kill | Creates a second action economy | None |

Do not turn the ledger into a transcript of every passing idea.
