# Night Monarch — revised master

Standalone Soma Cruz–inspired class. Warlock is its chassis donor.

Use [the revised PDF](Night-Monarch-Revised-Master.pdf) or [the editable Word copy](Night-Monarch-Revised-Master-Editable.docx). The old Draft filenames contain identical current copies to preserve existing download links.

The 28-page edition uses the supplied 35-page Night_Monarch_Revised_Master(1).pdf as its main authority, including its integrated identity and subsystem corrections. It replaces the previous 70-page layout based on the older upload.

The revised source resolves d8 Hit Dice, Master of Night, Dark Lore and Magatama rules; cut Armament and Abysscraft remain cut. Earlier source material restores the omitted Asura, Jaguar Sun and Rakshasa talent text. No full Unison Device rules were available, and none were invented.

The author’s session instruction removes the blood-point economy and dependent talents. Optional blood-point clauses were removed from otherwise functional talents. Full changes are recorded in Current Edition Notes.

Validation checked 80 progression rows, rendered content, text bounds, removal of blood-point rules and the Word archive. Remaining mechanical tasks are identified in the integrated revision and edition notes; document validation does not establish completed game balance.
