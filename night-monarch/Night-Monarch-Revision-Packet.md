## Crimson Elixir — author-confirmed restoration

Reference intent: Warhammer Fantasy vampire elixir. This reference explains the feature’s inspiration; the following mechanics are the author’s Night Monarch ruling, not a claim about published Warhammer rules.

**Crimson Elixir:** The Night Monarch has a number of uses of Crimson Elixir per day equal to her Charisma modifier. Each use produces an elixir that, when consumed, heals the target as *cure light wounds*. Alternatively, the elixir grants the consuming creature the **vampire template**. Crimson Elixir has no blood-point cost.

The daily allowance and both alternatives are confirmed. Restore this as an Infernal Art rather than treating blood-point removal as authorization to delete it. Do not add a separate transformation resource or charge without author instruction.

Production note: creation action, elixir storage duration, caster level for healing, and the exact vampire-template source/eligibility were not specified in these confirmations. Preserve any authoritative existing definitions if recovered; otherwise record these details for resolution rather than silently selecting them. This does not reopen the confirmed daily allowance or either effect.

Genie Bond

Sand Monarch Companion System · Pathfinder 1e

The Genie Bond represents the Sand Monarch's lifelong partnership with a unique spirit entity — not a djinn, efreeti, marid, or shaitan, but something rarer and more personal. The genie is always humanoid in appearance and present at the Monarch's side in court, in battle, and in private. Think of her as a companion first and a power source second.

The genie companion is separate from the Guardian system entirely. It does not occupy a Guardian slot, does not follow Guardian rules, and does not interact with Guardian class features. The two systems run in parallel.

Baseline Statistics

Vessel and Death

Vessel: The genie is anchored to a physical vessel — a ring, lamp, talisman, or similar object chosen when the bond is first formed. The genie may retreat into this vessel as a swift action and emerge from it as a move action. While inside the vessel the genie is inert and cannot act, but is fully protected from the outside world.

If the Genie is Reduced to 0 HP: The genie retreats to its vessel and cannot be permanently killed while the vessel exists. After one week of recovery the genie returns at full HP. The genie cannot be permanently destroyed by combat damage alone.

If the Vessel is Destroyed: The genie becomes dormant. The Monarch may craft a new vessel with one week of work and appropriate materials, at which point the genie awakens in the new vessel. The genie's personality, memories, and progression are fully preserved.

Genie Companion Progression

Core Features

All of the following are gained automatically and require no talent selection.

Genie's Bounty (Su)

At 1st level the genie grants its master access to all Genie Talents it possesses. The Monarch may use any talent the genie knows, though some talents require the genie's active cooperation. Talents that grant spell-like abilities or transformative effects use the genie's statistics for determining their effects unless otherwise noted.

Spiritual Development (Su)

The genie channels its essence into the Monarch, granting an enhancement bonus to either Strength or Dexterity. This bonus is shown in the Str/Dex Boost column of the progression table.

• Daily Choice: The Monarch chooses each morning whether the bonus applies to Strength or Dexterity.

• Split Bonus (9th level): Beginning at 9th level the Monarch may divide the boost between Strength and Dexterity in any combination totaling the listed value.

Alertness (Ex)

While the genie is within 30 feet of its master and not inside the vessel, the Monarch gains the Alertness feat, providing a +2 bonus on Perception and Sense Motive checks. If the Monarch already has Alertness, the bonus increases to +4.

Cultivated Power (Su)

At 3rd level the genie chooses an elemental affinity: fire, cold, electricity, or acid. This choice is permanent and defines the genie's elemental identity.

• The Monarch's weapon attacks deal bonus elemental damage of the chosen type. This damage starts at +1d6 at 3rd level and increases at 9th level (+2d6), 15th level (+3d6), and 20th level (+4d6).

• Outside of combat the genie can use its affinity for creative and narrative purposes — conjuring warmth, cooling the air, creating light from a spark, or soothing desert winds.

Elements

Beginning at 2nd level the genie gains elemental affinity slots called Elements, shown in the progression table. These slots represent the genie's deepening mastery of its elemental nature and may be spent to activate additional elemental effects beyond Cultivated Power's baseline damage.

Element slots refresh each day. The specific elemental effects available are determined by the GM and player collaboratively based on the genie's chosen affinity. General guidelines:

Genie Ascension (Su)

At 20th level the genie becomes a near-divine entity, greatly enhancing both its own power and the Monarch's influence. The following effects apply permanently:

• Wish: The genie may use wish as a spell-like ability once per day, using the genie's HD as its caster level. This is an alternative access point — non-Sand Monarch classes that bond with a genie companion also gain this ability through the genie at level 20 rather than through a class feature.

• Immortality Aura: The genie grants the Monarch immunity to fear and charm effects.

• Divine Presence: The Monarch gains a +4 bonus to all Charisma-based skill checks.

Genie Talents

At 2nd level and every even level thereafter the genie selects one talent from the list below. All talents may only be selected once unless otherwise noted. The Monarch may use any talent the genie possesses via Genie's Bounty.

1. Eye Candy — The genie radiates supernatural allure. Grants a +4 bonus on Diplomacy and Bluff checks. Once per day the genie may create dazzling illusions in combat, imposing a -2 penalty on enemy attack rolls for 1d4 rounds (Will save negates, DC 10 + 1/2 genie HD + Cha modifier).

2. Companion of the Lonely — The genie grants the Companion of the Lonely vigilante talent. The Monarch also gains a +2 morale bonus on saves against fear effects.

3. Sisterhood of the Master — The genie harmonizes with all the Monarch's bonded companions. Grants a +1 bonus on Aid Another checks and a +1 insight bonus to AC when an ally is adjacent.

4. Initiate Magic — The genie channels its supernatural nature into spell-like abilities. The genie gains access to a number of genie-specific supernatural abilities (elemental bursts, planar awareness, spirit sight) determined by its elemental affinity and HD. These are not drawn from the wizard/sorcerer spell list but are native genie powers. Specific abilities are defined collaboratively by player and GM based on the genie's affinity and narrative.

5. Garden of Delight — Once per day the genie creates an extradimensional sanctuary lasting up to 8 hours. Allies inside the sanctuary gain a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks and recover hit points at twice the normal rate during rest.

6. Eternal Promise — Once per day the genie may activate one of the following as an immediate action: absorb one attack targeting the Monarch (negating it entirely); grant the Monarch a +2 bonus to all rolls for 1 round; or heal the Monarch for 1d8 + genie HD hit points, taking the same amount as nonlethal damage itself.

7. Crest of the Magic Lord — Minimum genie level 16. The genie channels its near-divine power directly into the Monarch's casting, granting an additional spell slot of a level equal to 1/3 the genie's HD (maximum 5th level). This slot refreshes each day and may be used for any Spheres of Power effect or prepared spell the Monarch knows.

8. Awareness — The genie deepens its perceptual bond with the Monarch. Grants Alertness (stacks with existing bonus), Empathic Link (the Monarch is always aware of the genie's general emotional state regardless of distance), and Share Spells (spells with a target of 'you' may be cast on the genie while it is within 30 ft.).

9. Cultivator — The genie's Spiritual Development bonus may apply to Wisdom and Charisma instead of Strength and Dexterity. The daily choice now includes all four ability scores.

10. Master's New Clothes — The genie enhances the Monarch's appearance with subtle magical glamour. Grants a +2 bonus to Bluff, Diplomacy, and Disguise checks. The genie may also cast disguise self once per day on the Monarch as a spell-like ability.

11. Magi Change (Weapon) — The genie transforms itself into a magical weapon sized for the Monarch. The weapon has an enhancement bonus equal to half the genie's HD (minimum +1, maximum +5) and deals bonus elemental damage matching the genie's affinity. The weapon form may be any weapon the Monarch is proficient with, chosen when this talent is selected. The genie may revert to its normal form as a swift action.

12. Magi Change (Armor) — The genie transforms itself into a suit of magical armor sized for the Monarch. The armor has an enhancement bonus equal to half the genie's HD (minimum +1, maximum +5) and grants energy resistance 10 against the genie's elemental affinity. The armor type is chosen when this talent is selected. The genie may revert to its normal form as a swift action.

13. Slotted Magi Change — Prerequisite: Magi Change (Weapon) or Magi Change (Armor). Enhances the Magi Change forms with magical enhancement properties (equivalent to weapon/armor special abilities) chosen when the talent is selected, with a total bonus value not exceeding 1/4 the genie's HD.

14. Transportation — The genie unlocks the Carpet of the Sand Monarch companion system, functioning as an alternative entry point equivalent to the Carpet monarch art. If the Monarch already has the Carpet art, this talent instead grants the carpet a permanent +10 ft. speed increase. See the Carpet of the Sand Monarch document for full rules.

15. Genie's Veils — The genie gains the ability to bind veils using Charisma as its veil-weaving ability score. The genie may bind a number of veils equal to 1/4 its HD, accessing the veil list appropriate to its nature. [Akashic Mysteries]

16. Akashic Understanding — Prerequisite: Genie's Veils, genie level 8. Upgrades the genie to a full veil weaver with access to all standard chakra slots. The genie's essence pool equals its HD. [Akashic Mysteries]

17. Genie Blow — The genie delivers a massive elemental strike as a natural weapon attack. Unison device

Unison Device (Su)

At 1st level the warlock is partnered with a Unison Device, a tiny intelligent magical construct or spirit that acts as both assistant and amplifier of the warlock’s power.

The device typically appears as a pixie-sized entity, floating object, or small spirit avatar. It possesses its own personality and may communicate verbally or telepathically with its master.

The Unison Device functions as a companion using the progression shown on the Unison Device table.

The device has the following base traits:

• Size: Tiny

• Type: Construct or Outsider (spirit)

• Intelligence: 10

• Languages: understands its master

• Speed: fly 30 ft (perfect)

The device normally exists in Standby Mode, but may merge with its master using Unison Mode.

Standby Mode

While in standby, the Unison Device acts independently and occupies its own space.

In this state it provides passive assistance to the warlock and grants the following benefits:

• Alertness feat while within 30 ft

• may communicate with the warlock at any distance on the same plane

• may deliver touch spells for the warlock

The device is not primarily a combatant and rarely makes attacks.

Unison Mode

As a standard action the warlock may merge with their Unison Device, entering Unison Mode.

While merged:

• the device occupies the warlock’s space

• the warlock gains the Strength/Dexterity bonus listed on the table

• the warlock gains access to Unison talents that function only during Unison Mode

Unison Mode lasts a number of rounds equal to the warlock’s caster level.

Ending Unison Mode is a free action.

If the warlock becomes unconscious the Unison immediately ends.

Barrier System

When entering Unison Mode the device generates a magical barrier protecting the warlock.

Barrier Strength =

warlock level × 2 + Charisma modifier

Barrier strength functions as temporary hit points.

Damage is deducted from the barrier before affecting the warlock.

When the barrier reaches 0 it collapses.

Barrier strength is restored when Unison Mode ends.

Unison Bounty (Su)

While in Unison Mode, the warlock gains the benefits of all Unison talents possessed by the Unison Device. If a talent would normally require an action from the device, the warlock may instead perform that action.

Talents that modify the barrier, synchronization, or spellcasting apply directly to the warlock while merged.

Once Unison Mode ends, the device regains control of its talents.

Unison Talent

If the warlock becomes unconscious, stunned, or otherwise unable to act while the Unison Device is present, the device may immediately assume control of the warlock’s body as an immediate action.

Puppet Mode lasts for a number of rounds equal to the device’s level or until the warlock regains consciousness.

Eyecandy (Ex): The Succubus’s infernal allure grows stronger. She gains a +2 inherent bonus to Charisma. This bonus increases to +4 at 10th level.

Initiate Magic (Sp): The Succubus gains access to the spell-like abilities of a standard Succubus from the Pathfinder Bestiary. She may use detect good, charm person, and suggestion as spell-like abilities a number of times per day equal to 3 + her Charisma modifier.

Magi-Change: Succubus Arms (Su) [Magi-Change]: The Succubus can transform herself into a weapon for her master. As a full-round action, she may transform into a melee or ranged weapon, granting the following benefits:

Base Damage: The weapon chosen mirrors a standard weapon of her master’s choice, scaling appropriately for size.

Enhancement Bonus: The weapon gains an enhancement bonus equal to half her Hit Dice (minimum +1, maximum +5).

Special Property: The weapon deals damage as if it were evil-aligned for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.

The transformation lasts for a number of rounds equal to the Succubus’s Hit Dice or until she reverts as a free action.

Upgrades: With the Fast Magi-Change talent, this becomes a standard action, and with Lightning Magi-Change, it becomes a swift action.

Magi-Change: Armor of Lust (Su) [Magi-Change]: The Succubus transforms into magical armor, enveloping her master. As a full-round action, she may take the form of light, medium, or heavy armor.

Enhancement Bonus: The armor grants an enhancement bonus to AC equal to half her Hit Dice (minimum +1, maximum +5).

Special Properties:

Provides fire resistance 10.

Grants a +2 bonus to saves against mind-affecting effects.

The transformation lasts for a number of rounds equal to her Hit Dice or until she reverts as a free action.

Upgrades: With the Fast Magi-Change talent, this becomes a standard action, and with Lightning Magi-Change, it becomes a swift action.

Fast Magi-Change (Su) [Magi-Change]: The Succubus learns to streamline her transformations. Activating her Magi-Change abilities now requires only a standard action, rather than a full-round action.

Lightning Magi-Change (Su) [Magi-Change]: The Succubus perfects her ability to transform rapidly. Activating her Magi-Change abilities now requires only a swift action, rather than a standard action. Prerequisite: Fast Magi-Change.

Slotted Magi-Change (Su) [Magi-Change]: When the Succubus transforms into a weapon or armor, she creates slots that her master can use to enhance the form. The master may apply spheres or talents related to the transformed form for the duration.

Sisterhood of the Master (Ex): The Succubus Companion can share her master with other female companions, binding herself to the same master bond. While in the presence of another female bonded companion, the Succubus gains a +2 morale bonus to all saves and increases her effective Hit Dice by 2 for the purposes of ability scaling.

Companion of the Lonely (Ex): The Succubus gains the Vigilante’s Social Talent: Loyal Aid. She gains a +4 bonus on Diplomacy checks made to gather information and may deliver messages and warnings with uncanny speed.

Light Halo (Su)

As a standard action, the Angeloid fires a crescent-shaped blast in a 30-foot line, dealing 1d6 radiant damage per two Hit Dice (max 10d6).

The beam extends +5 feet per level beyond the base range.

Level 10: The Angeloid can fire two halos per action.

Level 15: The halos explode on impact, dealing half damage in a 10-foot radius.

Light Spear (Su)

As a standard action, the Angeloid hurls a divine spear at a target within 60 feet, dealing 1d8 radiant damage per two Hit Dice (max 10d8).

The spear bypasses all damage reduction except DR/-.

Level 12: The Angeloid can summon two spears per action.

Level 18: The spears explode on impact, dealing half damage in a 10-foot radius.

The genie can cast one spell per day of up to 9th level (sorcerer/wizard spell list).

Demon of Multiple Worlds (Ex): The Succubus gains access to magical effects from other settings. She may select up to three supernatural or spell-like abilities thematically appropriate to the campaign world and use each once per day.
